All of them at once

Fifteen competing definitions of who or what belongs where, projected onto the same nine territories. They are not the same species of thing: some are geography, some are membership of an organisation, one is a legal status held by a person. Pick a row to put it on the map.

02

Designation of persons

Non-binding. Binding in Belfast.

Boundaries govern territory. Eligibility rules govern people. Choose a place of connection and the context in which the question was put. Every formal ruling below is one you can look up, though sporting answers assume the person otherwise satisfies the relevant citizenship, ancestry, residence, prior-representation and selection rules. Birth alone has not been sufficient for British citizenship since 1983, or for Irish citizenship since 2005.

04

Known and unresolved

All verified. None defects.

Recorded here so they are not reported twice. No attempt should be made to reconcile them.

  1. The United Kingdom includes Northern Ireland. Great Britain does not. Team GB does.
  2. The British Isles include Ireland, which is not British.
  3. England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are conventionally described as the four constituent countries. In international relations the sovereign state is the United Kingdom.
  4. British Overseas Territories are British, are overseas, and are not part of Britain.
  5. Ireland names an island, and a state that does not cover the whole island.
  6. Team GB fields people from outside Great Britain and, in the Crown Dependencies, from outside the United Kingdom.
  7. One team at the Olympics. Eight at the Commonwealth Games, from the same eight territories shown here.
  8. In rugby, Northern Ireland is Ireland. In football it is Northern Ireland. At the Olympics it is whichever the athlete selected.
  9. In cricket the board is the England and Wales Cricket Board, and the team is England.
  10. In the Ryder Cup, Britain is Europe. In the Team Cup, Britain and Ireland play against Continental Europe. Britain is Europe except when it is playing Europe.
  11. In these golf cups Britain is never simply Britain: it is Europe in the Ryder Cup, and Great Britain and Ireland in the Walker Cup and the Team Cup. At the Olympics it is Team GB.
  12. The Isle of Man has been an ICC member since 2004 and has never had an Olympic team of its own.
  13. Gibraltar has a football team recognised by FIFA and no Eurovision entry.
05

Notices to Mariners

Corrections issued. Chart never fully corrected.

Charts are amended by notice rather than reprint. So is the constitution. Both keep the errors of every previous edition.

  • 1707
    NtM 1707/01

    England, already merged with Wales, unions with Scotland on 1 May 1707. New name: Great Britain. Wales is not in the name, and is not mentioned in it again.

  • 1801
    NtM 1801/01
    Boundary amended

    Union with Ireland. New name: the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Ireland. All existing references to Great Britain remain in service and are now imprecise.

  • 1922
    NtM 1922/01
    Boundary amended

    The Irish Free State is established on 6 December. Northern Ireland exercises its treaty right to opt out the following day. The resulting state contains twenty-six counties. The United Kingdom's name is not corrected until 1927, when Northern Ireland is appended. The abbreviation is never corrected at all.

  • 1998
    NtM 1998/04

    Belfast Agreement. It does not invent anything: it recognises and guarantees the existing birthright of the people of Northern Ireland to be accepted as British or Irish, or both, and to hold both citizenships. The Authority regards this as the only part of the chart that works as drawn.

  • 2020
    NtM 2020/12

    Withdrawal from the European Union. Team Europe is unaffected, because Ryder Cup eligibility never ran on European Union membership. Britain remains Europe in golf for reasons that predate the European Union and outlasted it.
